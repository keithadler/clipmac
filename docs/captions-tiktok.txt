askmac.png — Ask for Mac
Free, open source Mac app: ask your Mac a question, get the answer and the file it came from. "How much was the lease deposit?" It reads your PDFs, mail, notes and screenshots ON the Mac. Safe: nothing uploads, no account, and the code is public. Press ⌥ Space in any app. Get it: github.com/keithadler/askmac

Free, open source Mac app that shows which apps talk to which companies, by name. "Spotify: Spotify, Fastly, Google Cloud." "MeetingBot: Mixpanel, DoubleClick."
